MITRE ATT&CK Threat Intelligence

A navigable reference to the MITRE ATT&CK® Enterprise knowledge base: how adversaries operate, who they are, the tools they use and how to defend against them. Every entry links to its MITRE page.

I

Why It Matters

  • Attackers repeat themselves. The same few hundred techniques appear in breach after breach. Know them, and defense stops being guesswork.
  • One shared language. “T1566 Phishing” means the same thing to your analyst, your vendor and your auditor.
  • Spend where it counts. See which techniques most groups use, and which defenses stop the most of them.
II

Who It’s For

  • Security analysts & threat hunters — turn an alert into “which technique, which group, what next.”
  • IT & network engineers — find the controls (MFA, segmentation, patching) that block the most attacks.
  • Managers, CISOs & business owners — understand who attacks organizations like yours, and why the basics matter.
  • Auditors & compliance teams — tie controls to real adversary behavior in risk assessments.
  • Students & certification candidates — CISSP, CCIE Security, Security+: study how real attacks work.
III

How to Use It

  1. Got an alert? Open Techniques and type its ID (e.g. T1110).
  2. Researching a threat actor? Open Groups and search any name or alias.
  3. Planning defenses? Start with Defend First below, then Mitigations.
